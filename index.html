Cecilia Seri

Hi!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at UNU-MERIT. My primary fields are the economics of innovation and firm dynamics, with a focus on market power. I also maintain research interests in economic geography and development.

My PhD thesis focuses on technological change, its relation with firms' strategies and countries' development, and the implications for changes in global leadership. My Job Market Paper empirically investigates the effect of technological acquisitions on the acquirers' markups.

I will join the European Commission's Joint Research Centre in Seville in December 2026 as an Economic Analyst in the Industrial Strategy, Skills and Technology Transfer Unit, where I was a visiting researcher in 2024–2025. I have also worked as a consultant for IDB Invest and UNIDO.
